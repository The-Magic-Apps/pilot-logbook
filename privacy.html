Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Pilot Logbook is a local-first iOS app for personal flight log keeping. This policy describes what data the app handles, where it is stored, and what is — and isn't — sent off your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The Pilot Logbook app does not run any analytics, telemetry, crash reporting service, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking code. We do not have user accounts on our servers because we do not operate any servers that store your data.

Where your logbook lives

  • On your device. Flights, aircraft, airfields, contacts, licences, medicals and the rest of your logbook are stored locally in Apple's Core Data store inside the app's sandbox.
  • In your iCloud account, if you choose. If iCloud is enabled in iOS Settings, Apple's CloudKit will sync your data between your own devices through Apple's infrastructure. We never see this data; only you and Apple do. To stop syncing, sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud for Pilot Logbook in iOS Settings.
  • In files you export. PDFs, regulatory logbook exports and backups created by the app are written to your device and shared only when you tap the share button.

What leaves the device

  • Airfield lookups. To fill in coordinates for airfields in your logbook (for the route map), the app may query public aviation references — currently aviationweather.gov and airport-data.com. Only the airfield code (ICAO/IATA) is sent; no flight data, no identity, and nothing is stored by us. These are one-off lookups, not tracking.
  • Subscription purchases. Auto-renewable subscriptions are processed by Apple's StoreKit. Apple receives the purchase information; we receive an anonymous receipt indicating an active subscription. We do not see your name, payment method or Apple ID.

Exports and sharing

When you tap the share button on a report, totals view, map or regulatory logbook page, the app renders a PDF or PNG into a temporary file and presents iOS's standard share sheet. Where the file goes (Mail, AirDrop, Files, etc.) is entirely your choice; the app does not upload it anywhere.

Exported PDFs deliberately contain no metadata that identifies you beyond what you choose to put in your logbook (e.g. your name on a regulatory page, when applicable). PDF Author/Title/Producer fields are left blank.

Free vs. paid

The export of your data — in every format the app supports — is available on the free tier. You always own your data and can take it with you, with or without a subscription.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, prompt you in the app on next launch.
