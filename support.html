Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and — if you have iCloud enabled — in your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. Pilot Logbook does not run any servers that store your logbook.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Sign in with the same iCloud account on the new device and CloudKit will sync your logbook over. If you don't use iCloud, use Settings → Data & backup → Back up logbook, transfer the file to your new device (AirDrop, email, Files), then restore it from the same screen.

How do I export my logbook?

Open Reports → Regulatory logbook, pick EASA or FAA, and tap Generate PDF. The PDF is multi-page A4 landscape with brought-forward / carry-forward totals on every page. Use the share sheet to send it by email, save to Files, or AirDrop.

You can also share any individual report or map via the share button in the toolbar.

What about sim sessions?

Simulator sessions appear in the regulatory PDF's synthetic training device columns, kept separate from aircraft flight time. An optional toggle on the export screen also records a session's PIC, dual, instructor and instrument time in the function columns.

Subscription and trial

Pilot Logbook is free to use for basic logbook keeping — the free tier stores up to 10 log entries and tracks one recency rule, and always lets you view and export everything. The Basic subscription unlocks unlimited log entries and recency rules; upgrade from Settings → Subscription → Upgrade to Basic. It comes with a 2-month free trial for new subscribers; after the trial it renews monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app before you subscribe.

Even without a subscription, you can always view and export everything you've already logged — your data is yours to keep.

Managing your subscription

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. From there you can change plan, cancel, or restart a subscription. Apple handles all subscription billing.

Restoring a purchase on a new device

Open Pilot Logbook, go to Settings inside the app, and tap Restore purchases. As long as you sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID used to subscribe, the subscription will be re-applied.

Why does an airfield not appear?

Pilot Logbook uses canonical aviation reference sources; we deliberately do not synthesise airfield positions from generic map data. If an airfield is missing, email us with the ICAO or IATA code and we will add it to the seed in the next update.
